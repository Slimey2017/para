Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information PARA uses, why it uses it, how connected services such as Steam, Google, and YouTube work, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: August 31, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the PARA platform, PARA Home, PARA Account, connected account features, creator features, and other PARA services that link to this policy.

PARA only collects or stores information needed to provide features you use, maintain account security, operate the service, and comply with applicable requirements. PARA does not sell personal information.

2. PARA Account information

When you create or use a PARA Account, PARA may process:

  • Your email address, display name, and PARA user identifier.
  • Account verification and sign-in status.
  • Profile preferences and setup choices that you choose to save.
  • Security and session information needed to keep you signed in.

PARA Account authentication is backed by Supabase. PARA does not intentionally store your account password in readable form. Authentication tokens used by the hosted web experience are handled through secure, HttpOnly session cookies.

3. Google and YouTube data

Connecting Google / YouTube is optional. When you choose Connect, PARA uses Google OAuth and requests the minimum scopes currently needed for the feature: openid, email, profile, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly.

With your authorization, PARA may receive and store:

  • Your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image.
  • Your YouTube channel ID, channel title, custom URL or handle when available, and channel image.
  • A snapshot of channel statistics made available by the YouTube Data API, including subscriber count, view count, and video count.

PARA uses this data to connect your Google / YouTube identity to your PARA Account, show your connected channel in PARA, and support user-facing creator features such as channel information and creator milestones if those features are offered.

Google token handling.

The current PARA Google connection uses the temporary Google access token during the OAuth callback to read your Google identity and YouTube channel information. PARA does not persist Google OAuth access tokens or refresh tokens in its public account metadata table.

PARA does not use Google user data for unrelated advertising and does not sell Google user data.

PARA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Steam account connection

Connecting Steam is optional. PARA uses Steam OpenID so that sign-in happens on Steam. PARA does not ask for or receive your Steam password.

When a Steam account is successfully connected, PARA stores the verified SteamID associated with your PARA Account and may store related public profile information if PARA adds that feature.

5. Optional diagnostics, personalization, and location

PARA's setup flow includes optional privacy choices for diagnostics, personalization, and location services. These choices are off unless you enable them.

  • Diagnostics: may be used for reliability and performance information.
  • Personalization: may be used to tailor suggestions based on activity you choose to make available to PARA.
  • Location services: allows supported apps or services to request location access where the platform supports it.

Availability and exact behavior may differ between the hosted web build and future native PARA builds.

6. How PARA uses information

PARA may use information described in this policy to provide account features, save preferences, connect third-party services, display linked account information, operate creator features, diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse, protect accounts, and maintain the security and reliability of the platform.

7. Sharing and service providers

PARA may transmit information to service providers only as needed to provide the features you request. Current integrations may involve Supabase for account/database infrastructure, Google and YouTube when you connect Google / YouTube, Steam when you connect Steam, and Render for hosted PARA services.

PARA may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect PARA, its users, or others from fraud, abuse, security threats, or unlawful activity.

8. Retention, disconnecting, and deletion

PARA keeps personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account and connected features, protect security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal requirements.

When you disconnect a supported Google / YouTube or Steam connection through PARA, PARA removes the corresponding connected-account record from its account database. You may separately revoke Google's authorization from your Google Account's connected-app settings.

During the current testing phase, requests to delete additional PARA Account data should be sent to the user support contact shown on PARA's Google authorization screen or other current PARA support materials. PARA may need to verify that the request comes from the account owner.

9. Security

PARA uses reasonable technical measures designed to protect user information, including HTTPS, secure account sessions, server-side validation of third-party authentication responses, access controls, and Row Level Security for user-owned database records.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your account credentials and revoke connected services if you believe an account or authorization has been compromised.

10. Children's privacy

PARA is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent where required by law. If PARA learns that personal information was collected in violation of applicable law, PARA will take reasonable steps to remove it.

11. Third-party services

Your use of Google, YouTube, Steam, Supabase, and other third-party services is also governed by those services' own terms and privacy policies. PARA does not control their independent data practices.

12. Changes to this policy

PARA may update this Privacy Policy as the platform changes. When material changes are made, the effective date on this page will be updated and additional notice may be provided where appropriate.

13. Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions or data requests during PARA's current testing phase, use the user support contact displayed on PARA's Google authorization screen or the current support contact published by PARA.
